# Decision Recall: Privacy Policy

Decision Recall is a Forge app for Jira, built for Atlassian Codegeist 2026 by Akshay Wagh (independent developer).

## What it stores
- Decisions you record on a Jira issue (text you type, the issue key, the time).
- Stored only in Atlassian Forge storage, inside your own Atlassian site.

## What it does not do
- No data leaves Atlassian. No external servers, no analytics, no tracking.
- No personal data is sold or shared with anyone.

## Deleting data
- Uninstalling the app from your site removes its Forge storage.
